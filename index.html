How TLC Pictures Began

TLC Pictures officially began on 23 January 2023.

Before TLC Pictures became what it is today, we were simply learning by doing. We filmed five practical videos while experimenting with filmmaking and trying different ideas.

One of those videos stood out. After talking about it with my sister at home, she told me she liked it, and the idea of Umlilo: The Fire began to take shape.

From that point, I created the TLC Pictures channel and posted the work there.

The name TLC came from a name people used to call me — Tender Love and Care. I kept the name, but gave it a new meaning for the filmmaking crew:

TLC — Local Crew Pictures.

We are local. We are a crew. And this is where our filmmaking journey began.

Emmanuel's Story

Emmanuel Malatji is an independent filmmaker and the founder of TLC Pictures — Local Crew Pictures.

His love for filmmaking began at the age of 13.

In 2020, he started creating comedy videos with a group he founded called Outline Boys.

The videos were simple and imperfect, but the experience became the beginning of his filmmaking journey.

Outline Boys ended in 2021, but Emmanuel's passion for creating stories continued.

Over time, his interest developed beyond comedy videos into storytelling. He began developing characters and plots, writing scripts, directing actors, operating cameras and editing his own productions.

Today, Emmanuel is a student while continuing to develop his craft as a filmmaker.

Through TLC Pictures, he wants to create meaningful stories, develop local talent and build a creative team around filmmaking.

His main focus is Christian filmmaking — creating stories that entertain, challenge, encourage and point people toward something greater.
